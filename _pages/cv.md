Skills
======
* **Modeling & simulation:** custom numerical solvers for coupled kinetic systems; stochastic simulation (Gillespie algorithm, continuous-time Markov processes); particle-based simulation with interaction potentials; all-atom molecular dynamics (microsecond-scale, Schrödinger); spin-dynamics simulation and forward modeling of experiments (Spinach)
* **Data analysis:** nonlinear least-squares and spectral fitting of physical models to noisy data; time-series spectral analysis (FFT, spectral masking, harmonic regression); residual analysis
* **Tools:** Python ([NumPy, SciPy, pandas, matplotlib]), MATLAB, Schrödinger, [Git, Jupyter, Linux/HPC]
* **Experimental:** solid-state NMR spectroscopy; experimental design and data collection on complex instrumentation

Independent projects
======

* **Spectral Decomposition of Midwest Power Grid Load** ([details](/portfolio/portfolio-1/)): separated thermal load response from behavioral periodic structure using FFT, spectral masking, and harmonic regression

Experience

  * Modeled the effects of rotational diffusion of a potassium-ion channel in a lipids membrane in order to develop methods to (1) detect this motion in sample and (2) decouple it from the effects of functionally relevant motions.

  * Led the course's Python-based computational lab section; revised labs and homework, wrote answer keys, and did all grading.

* **Undergraduate Researcher**, Keane Lab, University of Michigan, 2017–2020
  * Studied long noncoding RNA structure using solution NMR
