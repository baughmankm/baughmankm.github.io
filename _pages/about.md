**I build physical models and analyze messy environmental data to help address climate change.**

I'm interested in developing, modeling, and understanding complex systems in order to help people. That interest grew out of academic biophysics, and I'm now applying it to the fight against climate change.

My PhD work at Columbia was in solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy of proteins. It taught me to respect how difficult and messy it is to take accurate measurements on complex systems, and how much a realistically constructed model can do to make sense of that kind of data.

That is my goal moving forward: build realistic physical models, and use them to analyze messy environmental data, so we can address one of the most important issues facing humanity.
