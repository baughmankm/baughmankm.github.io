My interests lie in developing, modeling, and understanding complex systems in order to help people. This has interest has been based in academic biophysics, but now I am transitioning into applying this skill to the effort of combatting climate change. My background is largely in solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy, so I respect the difficulty and messiness involved in taking accurate measurements on complex systems. I also understand the power of using realistically-constructed models to make sense of these types of data. This is my goal moving forward: build realistic physical models for and analyze messy environmental data so we can address one of the most important issues facing humanity.
